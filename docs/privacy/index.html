Privacy Policy

Effective October 18, 2026 · Ergodic Theory is a product of Bayardelle & Co. LLC

The short version

  • No account, no name, no email. You never sign up. We don't know who you are.
  • Your portfolio stays on your phone. Your positions, holdings, trades, alerts and settings are stored on your device. They are never sent to us.
  • One random ID. To check that your subscription is active, the app sends a random, anonymous ID (your "Support ID") with each request to our server.
  • Apple handles payment. We never see your name, Apple ID, card or billing address.
  • No ads, no analytics, no tracking, no data sales. We don't use advertising or analytics tools, we don't track you across other apps or websites, and we don't sell or share your personal information.

The rest of this policy gives the details, including your rights and how to use them.

2. Information we collect

2.1 Information that stays on your device (we do not collect it)

The app stores the following only on your device, in the app's private storage (and, for the Lock Screen widget, in storage shared only between the app and its widget):

  • positions you enter (coin, side, size, leverage, entry price), holdings, portfolios and trades;
  • price, risk and funding alerts, and your settings.

This information is not transmitted to us or to anyone else. Notifications for your alerts are created by the app on your device; we do not use a push-notification service and do not receive a notification token. Reports you export (PDF or CSV) are created on your device and go only where you choose to send them.

2.2 Information we process to run the Services

What Details Why
Anonymous app ID ("Support ID") A random identifier created by our subscription provider, RevenueCat, when you first open the app. It is not derived from your name, email, Apple ID or device hardware. It is sent with each request the app makes to our server. To confirm your subscription is active and to help you if you contact support.
Subscription information Through Apple and RevenueCat: the product you bought, purchase and renewal dates, whether the subscription is active, the store country and currency, and transaction identifiers. To give you access to what you paid for and to handle restores, refunds and support.
Request information When the app or a browser contacts our server: the coins whose data is requested (for example, Bitcoin prices), IP address, approximate location derived from it, device and network information, and the time of the request. To deliver market data and forecasts, to keep the Services secure and working, and to prevent abuse.
Support messages If you email us: your email address, what you write, and anything you include (such as your Support ID, app version and device model). To answer you.

We do not collect your name, email address (unless you email us), phone number, contacts, photos, precise location, exchange accounts, wallet addresses, API keys, or the positions and holdings you enter in the app.

Our server learns which coins the app asks about (for example, it requests Bitcoin's price history and funding). Market data is the same for everyone. We do not store a list of the coins you asked about, and we do not link it to your anonymous ID.

2.3 Our websites

The website, the Ergodic Index pages and the free calculator use no cookies, no analytics, no advertising tags and no third-party trackers. Fonts are served from our own server. The calculator runs entirely in your browser, and the numbers you type are not sent to us. When you load a page, our hosting provider processes request information (2.2) to deliver it.

2.4 Children

The Services are intended only for adults aged 18 or older (or the age of majority where you live) and are not directed to children. We do not knowingly collect personal information from anyone under 18. If you believe a child has provided us information, contact us and we will delete it.

Legal bases (EEA, UK and Switzerland)

4. Who we share information with

We do not sell personal information and do not share it for cross-context behavioral advertising, as those terms are defined under California law. We have not done so in the past 12 months.

We disclose information only to:

  • Service providers (processors) that act on our instructions and under contracts that require them to protect it:
  • RevenueCat, Inc.: subscription management;
  • Cloudflare, Inc.: hosting, content delivery, network security and data storage for our server;
  • our email provider, for support messages.
  • Apple Inc. processes your purchase, billing and refunds as an independent business under its own privacy policy (apple.com/legal/privacy). Apple shares subscription status with us, not your identity.
  • Market data providers (such as CoinGecko): our server requests market data from them on its own behalf. No information about you is sent to them.
  • Legal and safety: to comply with law, a valid legal process or a government request; to enforce our terms; or to protect the rights, property or safety of users, the public or us.
  • Business transfers: to a buyer or successor in a merger, acquisition, financing, reorganization or sale of assets, subject to this policy.

Do Not Track and Global Privacy Control. We do not track you across websites or apps. Because we do not sell or share personal information, there is nothing for these signals to opt out of, but we honor them as a valid opt-out where the law requires.

California "Shine the Light." We do not disclose personal information to third parties for their own direct marketing.
