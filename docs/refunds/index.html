Refund Policy

Effective October 18, 2026

The short version

  • Ergodic Theory subscriptions are sold and billed by Apple, so refunds are requested from Apple, and Apple decides them.
  • You can ask from the app: Support & legal → Request a refund, or at reportaproblem.apple.com.
  • Cancelling stops future charges. You keep access until the end of the period you paid for.
  • We can't see or refund your payment ourselves, but we'll help: email info@ergodictheory.com.

1. Who bills you

When you subscribe to Ergodic Theory ($49.99 per month, auto-renewing) in the App Store, Apple is the merchant of record. Apple charges your Apple ID, issues receipts, and processes all refunds under the Apple Media Services Terms and Conditions. Bayardelle & Co. LLC never receives your payment details and has no technical ability to refund an App Store charge.

2. How to request a refund

  1. In the app (iOS 15 or later): open Support & legal and tap Request a refund. Apple's refund form opens for your current subscription.
  2. On the web: go to reportaproblem.apple.com, sign in with your Apple ID, choose Request a refund, and pick the Ergodic Theory charge.

Apple usually replies within 24 to 48 hours. If Apple approves a refund, your access may end when the refund is processed.

3. Cancelling

You can cancel at any time in Settings → [your name] → Subscriptions → Ergodic Theory, or in the app under Support & legal → Manage subscription. Cancel at least 24 hours before your renewal date to avoid the next charge. Cancelling takes effect at the end of your current paid period. You keep full access until then. Cancelling does not by itself refund past charges.

4. Partial periods

Unless Apple grants a refund or the law requires otherwise, payments are not refunded for unused parts of a billing period.

5. If something goes wrong on our side

If the app is unavailable or a feature you paid for doesn't work for an extended period because of a problem on our side, email info@ergodictheory.com with your Support ID (Support & legal → Copy Support ID). We'll work to fix it and, where appropriate, may give you free time on your subscription by offer code, in addition to anything Apple provides. If we permanently discontinue the app, we'll stop renewals, and you may request a refund of any remaining paid period from Apple.

6. Your legal rights

Nothing in this policy limits rights you have under consumer-protection law. If you are in the EU, the UK or another country with a statutory right of withdrawal for digital purchases, Apple handles that right under its terms. Where the law allows, by subscribing you ask for immediate access to the app and acknowledge that this may affect your right of withdrawal once access has begun.

7. Promotional offers

Free time from promotional offer codes has no cash value. It cannot be exchanged for money or refunded.

8. Billing disputes

Please contact Apple or us before disputing a charge with your bank. Apple can resolve most billing issues quickly. For anything else, write to info@ergodictheory.com.
