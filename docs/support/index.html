Support

Email: info@ergodictheory.com. We reply within 2 business days, usually sooner.

To help us find your subscription quickly, include your Support ID: in the app, open Support & legal and tap Copy Support ID, then paste it into your email. Tapping Contact support in the app does this for you, along with your app version and phone model.

Common questions

How do I restore my subscription on a new phone?

Sign in to the App Store with the same Apple ID you subscribed with, install Ergodic Theory, then open Support & legal → Restore purchases (also on the subscribe screen). Your subscription follows your Apple ID. There is no separate account or password.

How do I cancel?

Settings → [your name] → Subscriptions → Ergodic Theory → Cancel Subscription, or in the app, Support & legal → Manage subscription. Cancel at least 24 hours before your renewal date. You keep access until the end of the paid period.

How do I get a refund?

Refunds for App Store purchases are handled by Apple. In the app, tap Support & legal → Request a refund, or visit reportaproblem.apple.com. See our Refund Policy.

What is my Support ID?

A random, anonymous identifier that links your phone to your subscription. It contains no personal information. We use it to look up your subscription and referral rewards and to handle privacy requests. Share it only with us.

Will I lose my positions if I change phones?

Your positions, holdings, trades and alerts are stored only on your phone, for privacy. They are not backed up to our servers. iCloud or device backups of your iPhone may include them. Otherwise, re-enter them on your new phone. You can also export a PDF or CSV report under Portfolios & reports.

How do I delete my data?

Data you entered lives on your phone: Support & legal → Delete data on this phone, or delete the app. To delete the little we hold on our side (your referral record and support emails), email us your Support ID. See the Privacy Policy.

Why is the app's liquidation price different from my exchange's?

The app uses your entry price, leverage and a standard 0.5% maintenance margin. Exchanges use their own mark price, margin tiers, fees and funding, so their number can differ. Treat the app's estimate as a guide, not a guarantee, and always check your exchange.

How do referral codes work?

Open Invite in the app and share your link. When a friend subscribes and enters your code, you earn a free month as an Apple offer code. Tap Redeem next to it to apply it. Each subscriber can enter one code. See section 8 of the Terms of Service.

Is this financial advice?

No. Ergodic Theory provides estimates and information only. You make your own decisions. See the risk disclosures in the Terms of Service.

I found a security issue.

Please email info@ergodictheory.com with the subject "Security". We appreciate responsible disclosure and will respond promptly.
